About This Lesson

Improve a photograph with cropping, tonal adjustments, color correction, and careful retouching. The steps below use common Photoshop tools and panels; labels may vary slightly by version.

Learning Objectives

At the end of the activity, learners should be able to:

  • Identify and use the basic image-enhancing tools and features in Adobe Photoshop.
  • Improve image quality by adjusting resolution, sharpness, and applying the High Pass Filter.
  • Apply blending options to produce a clearer and more detailed image.
