Clone stamp

Clone Stamp Tool in Adobe Photoshop lets you copy pixels from one part of an image and paint them onto another. It's commonly used to remove unwanted objects, blemishes, scratches, or imperfections.

Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • Identify and use the Clone Stamp Tool in Adobe Photoshop to repair and edit photographs.
  • Remove unwanted marks, scratches, and damaged areas of an old photograph by copying pixels from a clean sample area.
  • Apply proper brush settings, sampling techniques, and editing adjustments to produce a clean and natural-looking photograph.
