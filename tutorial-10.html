About This Lesson

Create a short looping animation with frames, frame timing, and GIF export settings. The steps below use common Photoshop tools and panels; labels may vary slightly by version.

Learning Objectives

At the end of the activity, learners should be able to:

  • Identify and use the Timeline panel and animation tools in Adobe Photoshop
  • Create a GIF animation by arranging images into frames and adjusting their duration.
  • Preview, export, and save the finished GIF animation successfully.
