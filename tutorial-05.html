About This Lesson

Select a subject, refine its edges, remove the original background, and place it on a new scene. The steps below use common Photoshop tools and panels; labels may vary slightly by version.

Learning Objectives

At the end of the activity, learners should be able to:

  • Identify and use the selection tools in Adobe Photoshop to remove a portrait background.
  • Remove the original background and replace it with a new one using proper editing techniques.
  • Refine the edges and adjust the image to create a clean and well-edited portrait.
