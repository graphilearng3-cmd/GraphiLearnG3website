About This Lesson

Build a simple advertisement with a product image, headline, supporting details, and a clear call to action. The steps below use common Photoshop tools and panels; labels may vary slightly by version.

Learning Objectives

At the end of the activity, learners should be able to:

  • 1.Identify the basic tools and features of Adobe Photoshop used in creating a brand advertisement.
  • 2.Create an attractive brand advertisement by adding images, backgrounds, text, and other design elements.
  • 3.Apply creativity and proper design adjustments to produce a clear and appealing advertisement.
